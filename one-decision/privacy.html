One Decision Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Operated by SmartApp (smartapp-agency.com)

One Decision Uploader is a private, internal tool. Its only purpose is to upload videos that we produce to our own YouTube channel, One Decision Show. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside our team signs in to it.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner authorizes the app with Google, it receives an OAuth token for the YouTube Data API with these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload our videos and set their thumbnails.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to add caption files to our videos and read our own channel's basic details.

The app only acts on our own channel. It does not read, collect or process data about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube user.

How the data is used and stored

  • The OAuth token is stored only on our own production computer, in a file readable by the owner alone. It is not uploaded to any server or shared with anyone.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. On revocation, or on request, we delete the stored token.
